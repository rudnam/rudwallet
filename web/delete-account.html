Deleting your RudWallet account

You can delete your account for RudWallet (the Android app by rudnam, and wallet.rudnam.com) yourself at any time. You don't need the app installed.

In the Android app

  1. Open the Settings tab.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with your password (or, if you only sign in with Google, with your email address).

On the website

  1. Go to wallet.rudnam.com/app/settings and sign in.
  2. Choose Delete account and confirm it the same way.

If you can't sign in, write to francisalbarracin2@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, asking for your account to be deleted. Once it's clear the account is yours, it's deleted for you.

What's deleted

Your account and everything in it are deleted at once: your email address and password, accounts, records, budgets, categories, settings for each shop, the payment notifications and IC card history brought in, connection tokens, and every signed-in session. It can't be undone.

Database backups are kept for 30 days, so your data is gone from them within 30 days. Server logs (such as IP addresses) are kept only as long as needed to run and protect the service. Nothing else is kept.

Deleting data but keeping the account

You can delete records one at a time, or choose several and delete them together, in the app or on the website. Accounts, budgets and categories can each be deleted from their settings. What you delete is gone at once, and from the backups within 30 days.
