What it does

  • Payment emails become records

    Forward your card's payment emails to your private address. Each one waits under To review, and confirming it teaches Wallet the shop's category and the card's account.

  • Google Wallet, on Android

    The Android app reads Google Wallet's payment notifications. A payment reported by both an email and a notification is recorded once.

  • Budgets and a monthly overview

    See where the month stands at a glance: balances across accounts, spending by category, and how much of each budget is left.

  • Stats over any period

    Pick a week, a month, a year or your own dates, and see how your spending moves from month to month.

  • Bring your history

    Import PayPay's transaction history, or records exported from BudgetBakers' Wallet app, as CSV files.

  • Claude, with your own key

    Add an Anthropic API key to have Claude suggest categories for uncategorised records, or connect Claude Desktop and other MCP apps to your records.
