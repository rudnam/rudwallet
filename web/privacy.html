RudWallet

Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

RudWallet (the Android app and wallet.rudnam.com) keeps a record of your money for you. It collects only what that needs, uses none of it for ads or analytics, and sells none of it.

Who runs it

RudWallet is run by an individual developer, rudnam on Google Play. For questions about this policy or about your data, write to francisalbarracin2@gmail.com.

What it collects

  • Your account: your email address and password. The password is never stored as such, only as a hash that can't be turned back into it (PBKDF2). If you sign in with Google, the only thing RudWallet gets from Google is your email address.
  • What you record: your accounts and their balances, your records (amount, shop, category, date and time, note, tags), budgets, recurring payments, categories and your settings for each shop.
  • Payment notifications (only if you turn this on in the Android app): the title, text and time of notifications from PayPay, Google Wallet and Vpass, sent to the server to become records. Notifications from any other app are ignored and never leave the phone.
  • Transit IC cards (only when you read one): the card's recent history and balance, sent to the server to be imported as records.
  • Statement files (only when you import one): the contents of the CSV file you pick, sent to the server to become records.
  • Server logs: to run the service and protect it from abuse, the server logs requests (such as the IP address, time and the page asked for).

It doesn't collect your location, contacts, photos or your device's advertising ID. The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

How it's used

Only to show you your records, balances, budgets and totals, keep you signed in, send the emails that confirm your address or reset your password, and keep the service running safely.

Who else handles it

Your data isn't sold or shared for advertising. To run the service, these companies handle it on RudWallet's behalf:

  • Amazon Web Services, where the server, the database and its backups run.
  • An email delivery service, which gets your email address to deliver the confirmation and password reset emails.
  • Google, if you sign in with Google.

If you make a connection token in Settings › Claude, or connect Claude or another app, the app you connected can read and tidy your records. You can revoke it at any time.

Beyond that, it's shared with no one unless the law requires it.

How it's protected

Everything between the app or your browser and the server is encrypted with HTTPS. The app keeps its sign-in on the phone encrypted with a key from Android's Keystore. Your data can be seen only from your account.

How long it's kept, and deleting it

Your data is kept while you have an account. You can delete the account at any time in the app or on the website, in Settings › Delete account. That removes the account and everything in it at once, and from the database backups within 30 days. See Deleting your account for the steps. A demo's data is deleted by itself within a day.

Children

RudWallet is meant for adults and isn't directed at children.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, this page is updated and the date at the top changes with it.
